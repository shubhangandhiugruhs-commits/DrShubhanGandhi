Vision — Dr. SHUBHAN GANDHI, B.D.S. (FPFA, USA)

08 The vision

THE WORK AHEAD

Stronger health systems through prevention, evidence and responsible leadership.

The long-term direction sits at the intersection of public health practice, health-systems strengthening, implementation science and preventive oral-health policy. Clinical care supplies the questions; research, teaching and systems work create the route toward answers.

RESEARCH & SCHOLARLY WORK

Four peer-reviewed publications and a co-edited research book

Work spanning COVID-19-related oral health, cracked teeth in endodontic care, mental health in dentistry and innovation in oral healthcare.

ACADEMIC PRESENTATION

Four national and international presentations

Including first prize at Maulana Azad Institute of Dental Sciences and an invited Young Student Speaker presentation at ADOH 2023 in Dubai.

REGISTERED DESIGN WORK

India and United Kingdom

A foldable portable travel toothbrush registered in India and a solar-powered smart dental sterilisation tray registered in the United Kingdom.

DEVELOPING METHODS

Healthcare AI, epidemiology and implementation

Current advanced learning in healthcare AI and machine learning supports a broader goal: rigorous competence in epidemiology, biostatistics, programme evaluation and evidence-informed implementation.
