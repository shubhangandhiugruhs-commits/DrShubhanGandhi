# Dr. SHUBHAN GANDHI, B.D.S. (FPFA, USA) — Official Website

A dependency-free, multi-page professional website, photographic journal and complete credentials archive.

## Website pages

- `index.html` — photographic homepage and pathways
- `journey.html` — clinical and public-health journey
- `leadership.html` — leadership and recognition
- `global.html` — interactive global-engagement map
- `journal.html` — photographs and films
- `archive.html` — searchable 122-record evidence archive
- `contact.html` — email form and verified social links
- `vision.html` — professional vision

The signature is the homepage control and primary identity. The SG monogram is the compact identity mark. The personal seal authenticates the archive and footer; the Presidential Seal is reserved for leadership context.
